The DETECT method

DDiscover

Spot a claim made by an AI tool.

EExamine

Identify the concepts and what looks suspicious.

TTest

Check it against reliable sources and evidence.

EExplain

Explain why it is right, partly right or wrong.

CCorrect

Write a corrected, sourced explanation.

TTransfer

Apply the habit to new claims and daily life.

Teachers and tasks

Shared standard for every teacher

When a teacher completes an activity they are responsible for, they submit the following.

Document / evidenceWhat it must include
Activity PlanAim, learning outcomes, target student group, duration, tools and implementation steps
Student InstructionsWhat students will do, task sharing, submission format and deadline
Collaboration EvidenceJoint decisions, contributions and edits by students from different countries
Final ProductA completed, checked student product with sources cited
EvaluationShort feedback, a rubric or another suitable assessment tool
TwinSpace DocumentationActivity description, product link, evidence shared with proper permissions, and a short result
Task owners follow the preferences partners gave in the form. The implementation details under each task are suggested working methods and have not yet been approved by partners.
